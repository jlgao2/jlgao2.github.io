## Key Question 1: Robotaxi — Waymo volumes/cities, unit economics, Tesla status, China scale, public exposure, who loses

### Takeaway
Robotaxis are the physical-AI category that has most clearly crossed from demo to measured scale: Waymo was at ~500k paid rides/week in ten-plus US cities by spring 2026 (10x in two years) and targets >1M/week by end-2026, while Tesla began Cybercab rides in Austin in Sept 2026 and has 1.7M+ paid FSD customers. Unit economics remain unpublished by any operator; third-party models put Waymo near breakeven per mile, and Alphabet's Other Bets still lost $1.8B in Q2 2026. China's Apollo Go leads on volume (3.2M driverless rides in Q1 2026).

### Inferences
- Waymo is the one operator with a disclosed, independently observable volume curve (50k → 500k/week in ~22 months); the plateau in disclosure since April suggests either slower growth than the 1M target or a strategic pause in reporting.
- The structural edge is fleet scale + safety data + city-by-city regulatory permits (a capital-intensive, slow moat); vehicle cost has fallen ~3x per unit (Jaguar → Ioniq 5), which is the main lever toward per-mile profitability.
- Tesla's FSD business is already a measurable P&L line (1.7M+ paid customers, >75% attach on new NA deliveries) even if unsupervised robotaxi is early (~2M miles, one city). The public-exposure contrast: GOOGL (Waymo buried in Other Bets losses) vs TSLA (FSD revenue recognized, valuation already prices autonomy).
- Public pure-plays remain thin: Alphabet, Tesla, Baidu (BIDU), Pony (PONY), WeRide (WRD), Mobileye (MBLY, flat revenue), Aurora (AUR, ~$15M revenue), Uber/Lyft as aggregators. Driver economics are the loser: Waymo prices below Uber per mile and keeps 100% of fare vs 25–30% platform take.

- No operator-disclosed unit economics (cost per ride/mile) for Waymo, Tesla, Baidu, Pony or WeRide; Pony/WeRide Q2 2026 financials and breakeven claims not reached (search budget exhausted).
- Zoox volumes, Uber/Lyft AV partnership metrics, and lidar names (Hesai, Ouster, Luminar) not researched.

## Key Question 2: Healthcare — ambient scribe adoption and ROI, insurer AI and backlash, FDA AI device count, radiology, public winners

### Takeaway
Ambient scribes are the fastest-adopted healthcare technology ever (600+ orgs on Microsoft DAX alone; Epic shipped native AI Charting in Feb 2026) with consistent evidence of burnout reduction, but rigorous studies show modest time savings (~13–16 minutes/day; 1.8 min/visit at UChicago) and no proven financial ROI per PHTI. FDA's AI device list reached ~1,614 (76% radiology). Insurer AI (UNH's $3B push) is administrative and politically exposed; the only clear public winners are platform owners (MSFT via Nuance, Epic is private) and procedure-driven robotics (ISRG +14–15% procedures).

### Cited Findings
**Adoption**
- Microsoft: DAX ambient technology assisted >3M patient conversations across 600 healthcare organizations in a single month (March 2025 launch materials); 600+ organizations deployed as of 2026 reviews; GA in US, Canada, UK with European rollouts (Austria, France, Germany, Ireland Oct 2025; Belgium/Netherlands early 2026) — [Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2025/03/03/microsoft-dragon-copilot-provides-the-healthcare-industrys-first-unified-voice-ai-assistant-that-enables-clinicians-to-streamline-clinical-documentation-surface-information-and-automate-task/); [Commure review](https://www.commure.com/blog-scribe/dax-ai-scribe); [Microsoft HIMSS 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-cloud/blog/healthcare/2026/03/05/unify-simplify-scale-microsoft-dragon-copilot-meets-the-moment-at-himss-2026/)
- Epic released native AI Charting to EHR customers on Feb 4, 2026; commentary calls it an existential threat to standalone scribes (Abridge, Ambience, Nabla); pricing undisclosed — [HIT Consultant](https://hitconsultant.net/2026/02/05/epic-releases-ai-charting-ambient-ai-market-implications/); [MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2026/02/ambient-scribe-ai-startups-epic/)
- Abridge ranked #1 by KLAS in ambient; a competitor review claims deployment at Mayo, Duke, Johns Hopkins, Kaiser and 250+ health systems (marketing claim, unverified) — [Vero review](https://www.veroscribe.com/blog/abridge-review-2026); [MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2026/02/ambient-scribe-ai-startups-epic/)
- PHTI (Mar 2025): ambient scribes set to be the fastest technology adopted in healthcare; costs ~$100–$600 per provider per month — [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/phti-report-finds-ambient-scribes-set-be-fastest-tech-adopted-healthcare); [HC Innovation](https://www.hcinnovationgroup.com/analytics-ai/generative-ai/news/55277198/report-ambient-scribes-help-with-burnout-but-financial-impact-unclear)
- Adoption within systems is uneven: cohorts of heavy users, partial users, and low/no users including those who tried and stopped — [HC Innovation](https://www.hcinnovationgroup.com/home/article/55283221/ambient-scribe-physician-adoption-uneven-as-use-cases-evolve)

**Measured outcomes**
- JAMA multi-site study (five academic medical centers, ~8,581 clinicians; Ambience, DAX and/or Abridge): ambient scribes decreased total EHR time by 13.4 min and documentation time by 16.0 min (per day) — [Commure review](https://www.commure.com/blog-scribe/dax-ai-scribe); [BERI](https://www.beri.net/article/best-ambient-ai-scribes-health-systems-abridge-epic-ai-charting-dragon-copilot)
- UCLA Health RCT (238 physicians, DAX): improved burnout and task load, but 1.7% reduction in time-in-notes was not statistically significant (sample sizes conflict across secondary sources) — [Commure review](https://www.commure.com/blog-scribe/dax-ai-scribe); [TwoFold](https://www.trytwofold.com/compare/dax-copilot-review)
- University of Chicago (Abridge): note-writing time cut 1.8 min per appointment (15.9%) vs matched controls; no significant change in after-hours documentation — [BERI](https://www.beri.net/article/best-ambient-ai-scribes-health-systems-abridge-epic-ai-charting-dragon-copilot)
- Cleveland Clinic (Ambience): average time writing/reviewing notes in EHR down 14 min/day — [AHA](https://www.aha.org/aha-center-health-innovation-market-scan/2026-04-14-6-health-systems-enhancing-care-delivery-ambient-ai-scribes)
- PHTI: scribes decrease burnout and cognitive load but "have not proven a financial return on investment"; evidence of time savings limited; few peer-reviewed studies with mixed results; vendors extending into revenue cycle (strategy, not measured results) — [PHTI](https://www.phti.org/announcement/ai-scribes-reduce-clinician-burnout/); [Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/early-evaluation-ai-scribes-finds-decreased-burnout-limited-financial-roi); [Modern Healthcare](https://www.modernhealthcare.com/digital-health/ambient-scribes-peterson-health-technology-institute/)

**FDA AI devices**
- FDA list (Sept 4, 2026 update, decisions through June 29, 2026): ~1,614 AI-enabled devices; 1,553 (96.2%) via 510(k), 40 De Novo, 21 PMA; ~1,230 (76%) radiology; H1 2026 added 181 authorizations — [Complizen](https://www.complizen.ai/post/fda-has-cleared-1614-ai-medical-devices); [Sequenex](https://sequenex.com/fda-ai-medical-device-authorizations-analysis/); [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/fda-ai-medical-device-tracker)

**Surgical robotics (the clearest healthcare P&L line)**
- Intuitive Surgical Q3 2026: worldwide procedures +~15% (da Vinci +~14%, Ion +~31%); US +~10%, OUS +~20% (India +42%); 546 da Vinci placements vs 427; systems revenue +30% to $769M; total revenue ~$3.05B; FY2026 da Vinci procedure growth guided 14–15%; instrument/accessory price cut of 3–5% per procedure from 2027 — [Intuitive 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001035267/000103526726000071/isrg-20260930.htm); [BioSpace release](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/intuitive-announces-third-quarter-2026-earnings); [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4654979-intuitive-narrows-2026-procedure-growth-outlook-to-14-percentminus-15-percent-amid-i-and-a)

### Inferences
- Scribes: adoption is real and broad; the ROI case is burnout/retention, not throughput. Coding/revenue uplift is a vendor narrative that no independent study in these results quantified. - Insurer AI is quantifiable only as cost-to-administer; the regulatory/political moat cuts the other way (litigation, prior-auth rollback mandates), so AI is unlikely to show up as a visible margin line at UNH/CI/ELV.
- Radiology AI is 76% of the FDA pipeline but productivity data at the radiologist level was not surfaced.

## Key Question 3: Insurance and banking — JPMorgan, Goldman, Klarna, Lemonade, Progressive/Allstate, Visa/Mastercard fraud and agentic commerce, fintech lenders

### Takeaway
Banking has the most explicit disclosures: JPMorgan says AI savings (~$2B/yr) now match spend, 150k staff use its internal LLM weekly, 90%+ of CIB engineers use coding assistants, and discrete functions cut 30–40% of roles. Klarna's revenue/employee hit $1.24M with headcount -49% vs 2022 but it re-hired humans for service. Lemonade shows the cleanest insurance proof (gross loss ratio 60% vs 67%, LAE ratio 5%, >50% of claims automated), while Progressive/Allstate disclose only qualitative gains. Visa/Mastercard agentic commerce is launches and frameworks, no measured fraud reduction yet. AI lenders (Upstart, Pagaya) are growing 20–60% with profitability returning.
