# Valuation multiples, 2026 YTD performance and non-US AI exposures (as of ~Oct 23–26, 2026)

**Method note (read first):** WebFetch and curl were both blocked in this environment (DNS failure / proxy 403 on CONNECT for stockanalysis.com, finance.yahoo.com, finviz.com, macrotrends.net, marketbeat.com, companiesmarketcap.com and even news domains). Every figure below therefore comes from web-search extracts of those pages or of news articles, not from fetched quote pages. Consequences: (1) metrics are not from one consistent source/date; each row states its own source and date; (2) "forward P/E" definitions vary by provider (StockAnalysis, GuruFocus, Motley Fool, Yahoo, Morningstar "normalized"), and where sources conflict both figures are shown; (3) many YTD figures are from the most recent article that quoted one, which can be days or weeks old — the as-of date is given in each row; (4) "not found" means no search result gave the number; nothing is estimated. Where I did simple arithmetic (e.g., distance from a 52-week high from a quoted price and quoted high) it is marked "(calc.)".

Table abbreviations: fwd P/E = forward P/E; EV/S = EV/Sales; "52wk" = distance below 52-week high; n/f = not found.

## Key question 1 & 2: Which names trade at extreme vs reasonable multiples, and which groups have corrected 30%+ from highs (vs not at all)?

### Takeaway
As of late October 2026 the extreme multiples (>40x fwd P/E or >15x EV/S) are concentrated in consumption-priced software/security (PLTR ~80x EV/S and 85–131x fwd P/E; CRWD ~208x fwd / 49x sales; NET ~50x EV/S; DDOG ~23x; SNOW ~19–21x; MDB ~45–56x fwd P/E), a handful of infra names on trailing earnings (GEV ~75x trailing / 27–31x fwd; PWR ~63x trailing; LITE 35x–120x fwd depending on estimate vintage; COHR 127x trailing), and non-US "scarcity" names (Cambricon ~214x trailing; CG Power ~111x trailing; Delta Electronics ~52x trailing). Most AI-infra semis trade at ordinary multiples (NVDA ~22–25x fwd, TSM ~21x, AVGO ~19x, ASML ~25–27x, MU ~5–6x), and the de-rated seat-SaaS, IT-services, ad-platform and Chinese internet names are now cheap on forward earnings (ADBE ~10x, WDAY ~11x, CRM ~14.5x, ORCL ~15x, INTU ~15x, GOOGL ~17x, TTD ~13x, APP ~18x, TEP ~10x, Tencent ~14x trailing). Groups that have corrected 30%+ from highs: fragile infra financing (ORCL −63% from 52wk high, CRWV ~−56–62%, NBIS ~−47%), seat-priced SaaS (CRM/ADBE/WDAY ~−31–33% YTD, HUBS ~−47%, NOW ~−40%), AI-power/thermal (VRT ~−45% from May ATH, VST ~−31% from high), ad-tech (APP −59% YTD), defense drones (KTOS/AVAV ~−40% YTD), losers (CSGP ~−49% YTD, FVRR −35–38%, LMND −38%, GETY in Chapter 11), and Korean memory (SK Hynix −31% and Samsung −22% just since Oct 6, though SK Hynix is still +97% YTD). Groups that have NOT corrected: life-sciences/CRO leaders (MEDP −2%, LLY −4%, TMO −4%, DHR −7%, ICLR −8% from 52wk highs; VEEV +44% YTD), security (CRWD +120%, PANW +108% YTD as of late Sept), memory (MU +228%, SNDK +430% YTD even after a 20% October drawdown), and Teleperformance (at its 52-week high).
