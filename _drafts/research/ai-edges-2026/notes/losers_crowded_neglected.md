## KQ1. Measured losers: companies with revenue/traffic/earnings declines attributable to AI

### Takeaway
The clearest, quantified AI casualties are (a) consumer Q&A/edtech (Chegg revenue -48% to -51% YoY in H1 2026; Stack Overflow new questions down ~99% from peak), (b) call-center BPOs (Concentrix revenue -1.2% YoY in FQ3 2026 with a $1.05bn goodwill impairment and a guidance cut; Teleperformance at decade lows), (c) tax software (Intuit: Goldman estimates AI can do a return for $0.12 vs TurboTax's ~$162 revenue per return; 17% workforce cut), and (d) ad holding companies (WPP Q1 2026 revenue -6.6% reported). ## KQ2. AI-washing: companies touting AI without financial evidence; SEC enforcement; short-seller reports

### Takeaway
SEC AI-washing enforcement in 2026 has been small-dollar and aimed at frauds (GenesisAI, Cryptoaiml, TSAI Pro), not large public companies; the only public-company action remains Presto Automation (Jan 2025). No 2026 short-seller report by Citron, Muddy Waters or Fuzzy Panda against a listed AI company surfaced; the main documented skeptic is commentator Ed Zitron, whose predictions are disputed. Hard evidence of AI-washing at large caps therefore rests on earnings-call mention counts vs results, which I could not retrieve.
